Privacy Policy — Cosmic Bash

Last updated: August 7, 2026 · Versión en español

This policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application Cosmic Bash (package com.itpro.cosmicbash, the "App"). Cosmic Bash is a local-multiplayer party game directed to children and families. For that reason, this policy is addressed especially to the parents and legal guardians of players who are minors.

2. Plain-language summary

  • The App does not require registration or an account, and never asks for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, or location.
  • We do not collect or store personal information on our own servers: the App has no backend.
  • Game progress (coins, achievements, skins, the player names you type, and preferences) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Because the App is directed to children, ads are always non-personalized (no interest-based advertising, no profiling) and limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences).

3. What information is handled, why, and on what legal basis

a) Game data (local, never transmitted). Coins, progress, achievements, player names, and preferences are stored only on the device. They never leave it and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings.

b) Technical data collected by the advertising provider (Google AdMob). To load and measure ads, Google LLC automatically collects, through its SDK inside the App:

  • IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived);
  • Technical device identifiers and diagnostic/performance data;
  • Ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

The App requests Google's child-directed treatment (the COPPA "child-directed" tag). With that configuration, Google does not use the Advertising ID for this traffic and serves only contextual, non-personalized ads.

Specific uses of these technical identifiers (the only ones permitted): serving contextual ads, frequency capping, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention, protecting the security and integrity of the service, and legal compliance. They are never used for behavioral or profile-based advertising, or to contact any individual. These uses fall within COPPA's "support for internal operations" exception (16 CFR § 312.5(c)(7)).

Legal basis (Art. 7 of Ecuador's LOPDP): performance of the service you request by using the App, and our legitimate interest in keeping the App free through non-personalized advertising and in keeping it secure. We perform no processing of children's data that would require additional consent (no profiling, no personalized advertising). If any future processing required consent, it would be requested from the parent or legal guardian.

4. Children's privacy

Cosmic Bash is directed to children and families and complies with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. COPPA law (15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506 and 16 CFR Part 312), and Ecuador's Organic Law on Personal Data Protection (LOPDP):

  • We treat all users of the App as children: there is no age screen, and the child-directed ad configuration always applies.
  • We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, location, photos, audio, or any other personal data from players; the only data that leaves the device are the technical identifiers described in section 3.b, covered by COPPA's "support for internal operations" exception (16 CFR § 312.5(c)(7)), which does not require parental consent because they are used only for contextual ads, measurement, security, and frequency capping.
  • Ads are non-personalized and limited to content rating G (suitable for general audiences).
  • The App has no chat, social features, real-money purchases, or links to external content unsuitable for minors.
  • In Ecuador, processing personal data of children under 15 requires authorization from their legal guardian (Art. 19 of the LOPDP Regulations). If your child uses the App, we understand you have allowed them to use it under this policy.

Parents' and legal guardians' rights (COPPA § 312.6 and Arts. 21 and 24 LOPDP): you may contact us at any time to (i) review what information is handled about your child, (ii) request its deletion, and (iii) refuse to permit its further collection or use. Since we do not store personal data ourselves, we will handle these requests by coordinating with Google where applicable, or by showing you how to delete local data and reset the device identifiers. Write to itpro.devs@gmail.com; we respond within 15 days.

6. International data transfer

The technical data described in section 3.b is processed on Google LLC servers in the United States. The United States does not currently hold an adequacy decision issued by Ecuador's Superintendency of Personal Data Protection, so the transfer relies on appropriate safeguards (Art. 57 LOPDP): the Google Ads Data Processing Terms, a binding contractual instrument requiring Google to protect the data, together with the technical measures described in this policy (child-directed treatment, no personalization). Purposes of the transfer: those in section 3.

7. Data retention

  • We retain no personal data (we have no servers).
  • Game data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Technical identifiers collected by Google are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in section 3 — never indefinitely — according to Google's retention periods.

8. Security

All communication between the App and Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). The App uses only the Internet permission; it does not access contacts, camera, microphone, personal files, or GPS location. If a security breach affecting personal data ever occurred, we would notify the authority and affected users within the deadlines of Arts. 43 and 46 of the LOPDP.

9. Your rights

Under Ecuador's LOPDP (Arts. 8 and 13–20) you have the right, free of charge, to:

  • Access your data and information about its processing;
  • Rectification and updating of inaccurate data;
  • Deletion of your data;
  • Objection to processing — including, at any time, objection to direct marketing and profiling;
  • Portability of your data;
  • Suspension (restriction) of processing;
  • Not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling (the App performs none);
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without justification, through a procedure as simple as the one used to give it.

To exercise these rights, write to itpro.devs@gmail.com (we respond within 15 days). Children under 15 exercise them through their legal guardian; adolescents 15 and older may exercise them directly. If our response does not satisfy you, you may file a complaint with Ecuador's Superintendency of Personal Data Protection (spdp.gob.ec).

In practice: you can delete all local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android settings, and you can reset or delete the device's advertising identifier under Settings → Google → Ads (the App's ads are non-personalized anyway).
